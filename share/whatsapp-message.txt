 1. WhatsApp -> chat or group -> attach the video:  sumant-weds-nikita-reel.mp4  (optional)
 2. Paste the MESSAGE below. WhatsApp shows the invitation card (image + website) from the link.

 Places and maps are on the website, so they are not repeated in the message.
